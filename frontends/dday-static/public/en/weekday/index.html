    Which weekday do public holidays land on?

    There is no reason for weekdays to come out even. One first of January falls on the same weekday in two hundred countries at once.

    Counting the 2500 days off of 204 countries in 2026 by weekday, Fri leads with 617 and Wed trails with 161. 544 of them (21.8%) land on a weekend.

    Counted over 2026 for all 204 countries in the data. The unit is the same as on the rankings page — “dates carrying a public holiday” — so the seven weekday cells add up to the date counts there.

    A fixed date falls on the same weekday in two hundred countries

    1 January is a Thu and that single day is a public holiday in 197 countries. 25 December is a Fri and covers 178. Those two dates alone fill 43.7% of Thu and 28.8% of Fri.
