    Cambodia Public Holidays

    Statutory public holidays in Cambodia. See at a glance whether today is a day off and how long until the next one.

    Cambodia has 21 public holidays in 2026, 3 of which fall on a weekend. There are 15 long weekends of three days or more; the longest runs 6 days around Khmer New Year (11–16 April).
